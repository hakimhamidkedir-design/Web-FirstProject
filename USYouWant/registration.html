Welcome to the official Ethiopian Motorsport Association (EMSA) driver registration page. This form is designed for athletes who are passionate about motorsport and are ready to compete at a professional level. Before registering, please carefully review all the requirements and ensure that you meet the eligibility criteria listed below, as this will help us maintain fairness, safety, and high competition standards across all events.

Requirements

In order to participate in the championship, all applicants must meet the following conditions and must be prepared to provide valid proof where necessary during the verification process conducted by the EMSA organization.

  1. You must be at least 18 years old at the time of registration to ensure legal eligibility and physical maturity for competitive motorsport.
  2. You must have prior training or experience in rally, circuit racing, off-road driving, or any recognized motorsport discipline to ensure safety and competitiveness.
  3. You must be physically fit and medically cleared to participate in high-intensity motorsport activities.
  4. You must agree to follow all EMSA rules, regulations, and code of conduct during training and official events.
