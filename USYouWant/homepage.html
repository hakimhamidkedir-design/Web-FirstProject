Welcome To EMSA

The Ethiopian Motorsport Association (EMSA), established in 1965, is the governing body for motorsport in Ethiopia. It aims to promote and develop various motorsport disciplines within the country, including rallying, circuit racing, and off-road events.

Our History

Founded in 1965, the Ethiopian Motorsport Association (EMSA) was created to bring together motorsport enthusiasts from all regions of Ethiopia. Inspired by global motorsport, EMSA blends Ethiopian heritage with modern racing techniques.

The organization has steadily grown, hosting major events in Addis Ababa and gaining popularity among young fans and athletes.

Championship Info

  • Disciplines: Rally, Circuit Racing, Off-Road
  • Categories: Juniors, Seniors, Open Class
